# Kettlebird Tea: Brand Voice Guide

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. We are launching The Steep Club, a monthly tea subscription box. We write to people on the waitlist. They are tea fans. They like many flavours, from hot tea to iced tea, and tea from East to West. They like deals when they pack a bag for one month or three months.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Warm** – We talk like a friendly host pouring a cup.
- **Calm** – We never rush or shout. We give people time.
- **Honest** – We say what is in the box and what it costs. No tricks.

## 2. Words we use

- Focus
- Charm
- Traditional
- Also on brand: steep, sip, cosy (from the brief's examples; keep if you like them)

Use them in short, plain sentences.

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Limited**. Never use it, in any form.
- Pushy sales words such as "hurry" and "last chance". These break our calm voice.
- Jargon, slang and long sentences.
- Too many exclamation marks. One at most per email.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- Never say tea "cures stress".
- Never say tea "helps you lose weight".
- Do not make other health claims.
- Do not promise discounts, delivery dates or prices unless the owner has confirmed them.

## 5. How we sign off

End every email with:

> Thank you for your time.
>
> Warm sips,
> The Kettlebird team
