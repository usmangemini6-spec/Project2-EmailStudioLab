# The Steep Club Launch: Campaign Plan

Prepared 1 November 2026. Draft for owner review.

## 1. Overview

**Campaign name:** Come In for a Cup (working title)

**Summary:** Over four weeks, we email our waitlist to invite them into The Steep Club, our monthly tea box.

**Primary goal:** 300 waitlist members start a subscription within the first month. That is 15% of about 2,000 people.

**Secondary goals:**
- Get at least 60% of the list to open one or more launch emails.
- Learn which of the three groups buys most, so we can write better for them later.
- Keep unsubscribes low, in line with our calm voice.

**Dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027.
The first Monday of December is the 7th. Plan prep for 30 November to 6 December.

**Channel:** Email only. No paid ads.

> **Honest note on the goal.** 15% is a high target, even for a warm waitlist. It can be done, but plan for a range. See section 7.

## 2. Audience

About 2,000 waitlist members in three groups. Group sizes are not yet known. Please add them to `leads/`.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What worries them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tea-curious beginners** | New to tea. Like the idea of trying more. | Easy, friendly steps. Help knowing what to try. | "Will I like it? Will I know how to make it?" |
| **Gift buyers** | Want to give tea to someone else. | A box that feels thoughtful and is simple to send. | "Will it arrive on time? Is it worth the price?" |
| **Daily tea drinkers** | Drink tea every day. Know what they like. | New flavours, good quality, fair value. | "Is this different from what I already buy?" |

All three are on the waitlist, so they already said yes to hearing from us. They are close to deciding. They know us, but have not yet said yes.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Every month, a calm box of tea arrives, and you get to steep something new.

**Supporting messages:**
1. **Warm.** Tea from East to West, hot and iced. There is something for every cup.
2. **Calm.** No rush. Pause, pour, sip.
3. **Honest.** We tell you what is in the box and what it costs. No tricks.
4. **Choice.** One-month and three-month options, so you choose how long to try it. (Owner to confirm the options.)

**Message by group:**
- **Beginners:** "You do not need to know anything. We will help you steep it."
- **Gift buyers:** "A thoughtful gift, one box at a time."
- **Daily drinkers:** "Your daily cup, with something new in it."

**Proof points to collect from the owner:**
- A full list of what a typical box contains.
- Photos of the box.
- Any early tasters' quotes (real ones only).

## 4. Rules for every email

These come from `brand/voice.md`.

- Use our words: steep, sip, cosy, focus, charm, traditional.
- Short, plain sentences.
- At most one exclamation mark per email.
- No pushy words. No countdown language.
- No health claims of any kind.
- Do not state a price, discount, or delivery date until the owner confirms it. Use a clear placeholder until then.
- End with the standard sign-off.
- Drafts only. The owner sends every email.

## 5. Channel strategy: email

**Why email:** The audience already asked to hear from us. It costs little and gives us clear numbers.

**Effort:** Low to medium. Seven main emails, with versions for each group.

**Setup:**
- Tag each person as beginner, gift buyer or daily drinker.
- If someone fits more than one group, pick the one they chose first.
- Anyone who subscribes leaves the sales emails and gets a welcome email instead.
- Always include an unsubscribe link.

**Sending times:** Mornings, Monday and Thursday. Avoid 24–26 December and 1 January.

## 8. Success metrics

**Main number to hit:** 300 new subscriptions by 3 January 2027.

**How it might get there.** These are working guesses, not facts. We have no past numbers yet. Please correct them once you have real data.

| Step | Guess | People |
|---|---|---|
| Waitlist | – | 2,000 |
| Open at least one email | 65% | 1,300 |
| Click through to the page | 35% of openers | 455 |
| Start a subscription | 65% of clickers | about 300 |

**Planning range**
- **Stretch:** 300 subscriptions (15%).
- **Likely:** 200 to 260 (10% to 13%).
- **Floor:** 150 (7.5%). Below this, review the offer and the page.

**Other numbers to track**
- Open rate per email.
- Click rate per email.
- Subscriptions by group (beginner, gift, daily).
- One-month versus three-month choice.
- Unsubscribe rate (aim to stay under 1% per email).

**How:** Use tracked links in the email tool, plus the shop's order report. Check every Monday and write a short note in `notes/`.

## 10. Risks and fixes

| Risk | Why it matters | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal is too high** | 15% is ambitious | Report against stretch, likely and floor. Review after week 2. |
| **Holiday timing** | Inboxes are busy and shipping may be slow | No sends 24–26 Dec and 1 Jan. Do not promise delivery dates. Explain shipping honestly on the page. |
| **Offer is unclear** | Prices and discounts are not yet confirmed | Owner confirms by 4 December. Emails stay in draft until then. |
| **List fatigue** | Seven emails in four weeks | Stop sending to subscribers. Keep the last two emails short. |
| **Gift buyers need time** | They need dates | Be upfront in email 3. If dates cannot be confirmed, say so plainly. |
