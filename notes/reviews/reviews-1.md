# Sampler Box Reviews 1

**1. Hannah R. – 5 stars**
The Darjeeling First Flush was a real treat. Light, floral and clean. I have already bought a full tin.

**2. Marcus T. – 3 stars**
Nice range of teas, but the box arrived crushed at one corner. Two pouches had split and Assam leaves were loose in the box.

**3. Priya S. – 4 stars**
Loved the Masala Chai. Strong and spicy, just right with milk. I wish the sampler had given me more than one pouch of it.

**4. Doug W. – 2 stars**
Shipping took 11 days. The tea was fine, but I ordered it as a gift and it missed the birthday.

**5. Elena V. – 5 stars**
Jasmine Pearls unfurled in the cup like little flowers. My daughter thought it was magic. Beautiful box too.

**6. Tom B. – 3 stars**
The Lapsang Souchong was far too smoky for me. It tasted like a campfire. Others in the box were good, though.

**7. Aisha K. – 4 stars**
Good mix of hot and iced options. The Hibiscus Berry made a lovely iced tea. Instructions were a bit small to read.

**8. Graham L. – 1 star**
Instructions said to steep all teas for 5 minutes. My Sencha came out bitter and awful. Please give proper times for each tea.

**9. Mei Lin C. – 5 stars**
The Dragon Well was sweet and nutty. Fresh, too. I could tell the leaves had not been sitting for long.

**10. Rachel O. – 4 stars**
Earl Grey with real bergamot smell. Very good. Shame the pouches were so small, I only got three cups from each.

**11. Jim H. – 2 stars**
Box was nicely wrapped, but there was a lot of plastic inside. I expected better from a tea shop.

**12. Sofia M. – 5 stars**
Honest and friendly. The Rooibos Vanilla is now my evening cup. Arrived in four days.

**13. Callum D. – 3 stars**
Tea was good. The Chamomile Honey was a bit weak even after a long steep. The Keemun was the star.

**14. Nadia F. – 4 stars**
Great way to try new things. I would never have picked Genmaicha myself, and now I love the toasty rice taste.

**15. Peter G. – 1 star**
Received the wrong box. It had the autumn selection, not the sampler I ordered. Slow reply from support too.

**16. Lucy A. – 5 stars**
Pretty tins, lovely smells, and a handwritten note. Felt like a gift from a friend.

**17. Omar J. – 3 stars**
The Moroccan Mint was great but I only got enough for one pot. Would pay extra for a bigger bag of that one.

**18. Beth N. – 4 stars**
White Peony is gentle and soft. A calm cup after work. Brewing card was easy to follow.

**19. Stan P. – 2 stars**
Three of the eight teas tasted stale. The Ceylon in particular was flat and dusty.

**20. Yuki I. – 5 stars**
The Matcha came with a tiny whisk tip in the card. It worked! Smooth, no bitterness. Very happy.
