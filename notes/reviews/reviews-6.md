# Sampler Box Reviews 6

**1. Imogen H. – 5 stars**
Lovely from start to finish. The Darjeeling and the Jasmine Pearls were my best cups. Quick shipping too.

**2. Dennis L. – 3 stars**
Decent teas. The pouches were small, so I only got a couple of cups of each. Hard to judge a tea that way.

**3. Yasmin A. – 4 stars**
The Rooibos Chai was cosy and sweet. I would buy a full bag. The brewing card was clear and easy.

**4. Brian K. – 1 star**
It took 18 days to arrive and the box was flattened. I asked for a refund and am still waiting.

**5. Susanna V. – 5 stars**
I loved the White Peony. Light, honeyed, and gentle. The whole box felt calm and kind.

**6. Idris B. – 4 stars**
Good mix. The Assam and Earl Grey were solid. I would have liked a bit more Assam, since I drink it daily.

**7. Cathy N. – 2 stars**
The Hibiscus was far too sour, and the Lemon Ginger tasted like cleaning spray. Only the Ceylon was good.

**8. Mohan R. – 5 stars**
A great way to meet new teas. Tie Guan Yin and Dragon Well were new to me. Both lovely.

**9. Ella J. – 3 stars**
Teas were fine. The instructions said "steep to taste" for half of them, which is no help to a beginner.

**10. Stefan P. – 4 stars**
The Sencha and Genmaicha were fresh and clean. The tins were handy. Delivery was five days, which is okay.

**11. Rina D. – 5 stars**
My iced tea summer was sorted. Hibiscus Berry and Moroccan Mint were both brilliant over ice.

**12. Colin T. – 2 stars**
The Lapsang Souchong ruined the other teas. Its smell got into every pouch. Why pack it next to the Jasmine?

**13. Abena O. – 4 stars**
Warm and friendly brand. The Masala Chai was strong and spicy. I wish the sampler had a second pouch of it.

**14. Harry M. – 3 stars**
Good tea, but the box was bulky and had a lot of filler paper. Postage must have cost more than needed.

**15. Valentina R. – 5 stars**
A little treat for myself. The Rooibos Vanilla and Chamomile Honey were my evening cups. I slept well and felt calm.

**16. Declan F. – 4 stars**
Great Yunnan Gold. Sweet, malty, easy to drink. The Gunpowder green was rougher, but that is just my taste.

**17. Pooja S. – 1 star**
The Matcha pouch was open and the powder had spilled over everything. Green dust all over the other teas.

**18. Lewis C. – 5 stars**
Everything tasted fresh. The Keemun was smooth and the Oolong was floral. I have ordered a second box for my sister.

**19. Anna-Marie G. – 3 stars**
I liked the idea more than the result. Some teas were great and some bland. Shipping was slow, nine days.

**20. Jun W. – 4 stars**
A fun box. Peppermint and Earl Grey were the winners. Please add water temperatures to the card next time.
