# Sampler Box Reviews 4

**1. Natalie P. – 4 stars**
The Assam and the Keemun were both strong and rich. I would pay more for a bigger sampler.

**2. Raj M. – 5 stars**
Masala Chai, Darjeeling and Oolong were all superb. Arrived in two days. I told my whole office.

**3. Eileen C. – 2 stars**
The brewing card was confusing. It said "3 to 5 minutes" for everything, then "do not over-steep". Which is it?

**4. Josh F. – 3 stars**
Decent tea. The Genmaicha tasted a bit stale. The Sencha was good, though.

**5. Thandi N. – 5 stars**
The Hibiscus Berry iced tea was gorgeous. I made a jug for a picnic and everyone asked where it was from.

**6. Howard D. – 1 star**
Shipping took 15 days and the courier left the box in the rain. Two pouches were ruined.

**7. Mia L. – 4 stars**
Lovely idea. The Jasmine and the White Peony were my favourites. Labels were a bit hard to read in low light.

**8. Arturo G. – 3 stars**
I liked the Earl Grey and the Ceylon. The rest felt like filler. Would have preferred fewer teas and more of each.

**9. Wendy S. – 5 stars**
Cosy, calm and kind. The Chamomile Honey helped end my evenings well. Lovely note inside.

**10. Ali R. – 4 stars**
The Moroccan Mint was cool and sweet. I wish there had been more of it. It disappeared in a weekend.

**11. Penny H. – 2 stars**
The outer box was fine, but the inner pouches were thin and let air in. My Matcha had gone clumpy.

**12. Luca B. – 5 stars**
I had never tried Yunnan Gold or Tie Guan Yin. Both were a delight. Great way to explore.

**13. Dina W. – 3 stars**
The tea was good, the delivery slow, and the card was missing. No idea how to brew the Oolong.

**14. Chen Y. – 4 stars**
Dragon Well was fresh and sweet. Sencha was also good. The Gunpowder green was too bitter for me.

**15. Frank O. – 1 star**
Not for me. Everything tasted weak, even after I doubled the leaves. Felt like a waste of money.

**16. Cora J. – 5 stars**
So much care in the box. The Rooibos Vanilla smelled like a bakery. We shared it with friends.

**17. Ethan K. – 3 stars**
The Lemon Ginger was sharp and zesty, but the pouch had a tiny hole and some leaked into the box.

**18. Sunita D. – 4 stars**
Great for beginners. I learned the difference between Assam and Ceylon. Wish the card listed the steep time for iced tea.

**19. Paul V. – 2 stars**
Too much packaging for so little tea. Each pouch was about two cups. I wanted more of the Darjeeling.

**20. Maeve R. – 5 stars**
Honest, tasty, and friendly. The Lapsang Souchong was smoky and bold, just how I like it. Loved it.
