# Sampler Box Reviews 3

**1. Olivia B. – 5 stars**
A joy to open. Each pouch had a little story about the tea. The Moroccan Mint was my favourite.

**2. Patrick J. – 3 stars**
Teas were nice. The pouches were hard to reseal, and my Sencha went a bit dull after a week.

**3. Leila H. – 4 stars**
The Hibiscus iced tea was refreshing on a hot day. Brewing card said "cold brew overnight", which worked well.

**4. Simon R. – 2 stars**
Too many fruity teas for my taste. I wanted more traditional black teas. Only got one Assam and one Ceylon.

**5. Grace Y. – 5 stars**
White Peony and Jasmine Pearls both lovely. Soft and calm. Shipped quickly, arrived in three days.

**6. Walter K. – 1 star**
The tin lid was dented and the Earl Grey inside smelled musty. Not what I expected for the price.

**7. Bianca F. – 4 stars**
Great variety. The Genmaicha was my surprise favourite. Only wish the instructions covered water temperature.

**8. Dev P. – 5 stars**
The Masala Chai was the real thing. Cardamom, ginger and clove all there. I made it on the stove as the card said.

**9. Colleen M. – 3 stars**
Lovely tea, slow delivery. Nine days for a small box feels long. The Darjeeling made up for it.

**10. Felix G. – 4 stars**
Good start to the Kettlebird range. I would like to see a bigger pouch of the Yunnan Gold, it was gone in two cups.

**11. Rosa D. – 2 stars**
The Chamomile Honey smelled lovely but tasted of nothing. Others were fine. Felt a bit overpriced.

**12. Anil V. – 5 stars**
Dragon Well was fresh and gentle. I have been drinking tea for 30 years and this was a good one.

**13. Jo-Anne T. – 3 stars**
The box was pretty but it was far too big for the tea inside. Lots of paper and not much else.

**14. Max L. – 4 stars**
Peppermint and Lemon Ginger were both excellent. The Gunpowder green was harsher than I like.

**15. Daria S. – 5 stars**
I loved it. The tea notes on the card were fun to read and helped me pick what to try first.

**16. Tim O. – 1 star**
Instructions were in tiny grey print. I could not read them. Brewed everything wrong and wasted half the box.

**17. Karen E. – 4 stars**
The Rooibos Vanilla was soothing. Wish there had been two pouches. The rest was good too.

**18. Sam N. – 3 stars**
Teas were tasty but the Lapsang had a really strong smell that went through the whole box.

**19. Mirela A. – 5 stars**
A thoughtful box. Earl Grey, Oolong and Matcha all tasted fresh. Neat packing with no wasted space.

**20. George B. – 2 stars**
Arrived with a torn corner and a missing pouch. Support sent a refund, but it took ten days.
