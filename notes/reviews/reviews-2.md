# Sampler Box Reviews 2

**1. Fiona McL. – 4 stars**
The Assam was malty and bold. A proper breakfast tea. I would have liked a second pouch of it.

**2. Victor Z. – 2 stars**
Delivery was nearly two weeks. The tracking page never updated. The tea itself was decent.

**3. Hana B. – 5 stars**
Tie Guan Yin Oolong was the best surprise. Creamy and floral, and it held up through four steeps.

**4. Derek S. – 3 stars**
Good teas, confusing card. It said "use hot water" for the green tea. How hot? I scorched the Gunpowder green the first time.

**5. Amara O. – 5 stars**
Everything smelled so fresh when I opened the box. The Lemon Ginger is now a weekly habit.

**6. Chris E. – 1 star**
The box was soaked at one end. Rain got in and the outer pouches were damp. I threw two away.

**7. Laura P. – 4 stars**
Lovely selection. The Peppermint was crisp and bright. Only complaint is the labels peel off the pouches.

**8. Ravi N. – 5 stars**
First time trying Yunnan Gold. Honey-sweet and smooth. I am hooked and will order again.

**9. Jessica W. – 3 stars**
Mixed feelings. The Earl Grey was perfect, but the Hibiscus was too sour for me, even with sugar.

**10. Ben K. – 4 stars**
Good value for the price. Eight teas, all different. I wish the sampler listed the origin of each one.

**11. Tatiana R. – 2 stars**
Only one Darjeeling pouch and it was the best tea in the box. I finished it in three days. So disappointing.

**12. Kofi A. – 5 stars**
Friendly service, quick delivery, and the Ceylon was bright and brisk. Perfect with a biscuit.

**13. Sandra H. – 3 stars**
Brewing times were on a separate leaflet that fell out of the box. I had to guess for most of them.

**14. Isaac L. – 4 stars**
Sencha was grassy and fresh. Genmaicha was cosy. The Chai was a bit dull, to be honest.

**15. Mary-Ann D. – 5 stars**
Gave this to my mum. She loved the Jasmine and the little tin. She has asked for another.

**16. Hugo F. – 2 stars**
Arrived late and the Lapsang pouch smelled so strongly that all the other teas now taste smoky too.

**17. Zara Q. – 4 stars**
Pretty box, good teas. The Rooibos Chai was a nice caffeine-free find. A bit more of it would be great.

**18. Neil C. – 3 stars**
Fine, not special. The teas were decent but nothing wowed me. Packaging was plain.

**19. Ingrid S. – 5 stars**
The Keemun was rich and smooth with a hint of cocoa. I did not expect that. Wonderful.

**20. Jamal T. – 1 star**
Waited three weeks. Wrote to support twice with no answer. By the time it came I had lost interest.
