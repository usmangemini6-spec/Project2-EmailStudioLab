# Sampler Box Reviews 5

**1. Gemma T. – 5 stars**
The Earl Grey was the best I have had. Real bergamot, not perfume. The Jasmine Pearls were lovely too.

**2. Nick A. – 2 stars**
Delivery took 12 days. The Peppermint was fine but the Chai was flat. Not worth the wait.

**3. Farah S. – 4 stars**
Good range, good smells. The Genmaicha was toasty and warm. I wish the box had more than one pouch of it.

**4. Leo P. – 3 stars**
The brewing instructions gave Fahrenheit only. I had to look up the Celsius. A small thing, but annoying.

**5. Ruth B. – 5 stars**
Gave it to my dad, who only drinks builder's tea. He loved the Assam and the Keemun. Now he wants the Darjeeling.

**6. Kenji M. – 4 stars**
The Sencha was bright and grassy. The Matcha was smooth. Packaging was neat but quite plasticky.

**7. Alice D. – 1 star**
Two of the pouches arrived open. Tea everywhere. I got a refund but it took weeks and several emails.

**8. Tariq H. – 5 stars**
Beautiful flavours. The Yunnan Gold and the Oolong were standouts. The box felt thoughtful and well made.

**9. Becky L. – 3 stars**
Mixed bag. I loved the Hibiscus and Lemon Ginger. The Rooibos Vanilla tasted artificial to me.

**10. Oscar N. – 4 stars**
Tasty teas. The Ceylon was brisk and good with milk. Delivery was a bit slow, but fine.

**11. Lena K. – 2 stars**
The Chamomile was dusty and the Peppermint had stalks in it. Not what I hoped for from a small tea shop.

**12. Vikram J. – 5 stars**
The Masala Chai made my kitchen smell amazing. My wife and I had it every morning for a week.

**13. Joan E. – 4 stars**
A cosy gift. The tins were sweet. The only trouble was the brewing guide, which did not say how much tea per cup.

**14. Hassan R. – 3 stars**
I liked the Darjeeling and the White Peony. The Lapsang was too strong for me. A smaller sample would be enough.

**15. Poppy C. – 5 stars**
A lovely first order. Arrived in three days with a kind note. The Moroccan Mint was perfect over ice.

**16. Gus F. – 1 star**
The sampler was missing the Oolong that was listed on the website. No explanation and no reply to my email.

**17. Tess O. – 4 stars**
Good tea and good value. I wish the Dragon Well pouch had been larger. It was my favourite and I ran out fast.

**18. Ibrahim Z. – 3 stars**
Nice teas, but the box was dented and the outer wrapping torn. Inside it was fine.

**19. Clara W. – 5 stars**
Everything was fresh and well sealed. Calm packaging, no fuss. The Keemun was my top pick.

**20. Roy G. – 2 stars**
Confusing. The card said steep green tea at "just off the boil" but black tea at "boiling". I could not tell which was which.
