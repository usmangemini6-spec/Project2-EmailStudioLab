# Handoff: email 01 subject angle

**Beginners** want easy steps and reassurance. **Gift buyers** want a thoughtful, simple gift and honest dates. **Daily drinkers** want something new in their usual cup.

**Angle A: a calm daily ritual.** Subjects like "A calm cup, every month". It fits our calm voice. It suits daily drinkers best.

**Angle B: discovering new teas.** Subjects like "Steep something new this month". It matches our core message, "steep something new".

**My pick: Angle B.** "Steep something new" speaks to all three groups: beginners try, gift buyers share, daily drinkers vary.

**Why not A?** It leans on a daily habit, which only one group has. We can still use calm wording in the body.
